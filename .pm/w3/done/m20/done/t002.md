## Objective

Muse 1.4.13 reminder children stop cluttering every turn with raw 'reminderChild: …' cards.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Blocking reminders (`blocking: true`) render as a card titled from `reminderAgentId` (e.g. 'Muse reminder: verify') with a one-line explanation; non-blocking reminders send no card.
- [x] `ITEM_KIND_CONSUMERS.reminderChild` records the decision; unit tests cover both kinds.

## Evidence (2026-10-25)

Observed on Muse 1.4.13 with a real turn: `skill-reminder` (non-blocking), `goal-reminder` and `verify-reminder` (`blocking: true`). `isBackgroundReminder`/`reminderTitle` in `src/async-tasks.ts`; blocking reminders render as "Muse reminder: verify" with a one-line explanation, background ones send nothing (turn translator and restored task updates). Test: `muse-sdk-events.test.ts` "names blocking reminder children and drops background ones".
