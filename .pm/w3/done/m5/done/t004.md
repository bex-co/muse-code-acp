## Draft (awaiting user approval; not posted)

Target: `meta-models/muse-code-sdk` issues. Related but distinct: #55 (`WriteFailed` on every anchor with sub-agent lanes, 1.3.0) and #31 (Windows `WriteFailed`).

> **Title:** `session/fork` rejects every completed turn except the latest as `cutPoint.lastTurnId` (`forkBoundaryInvalid: InvalidCut`, 1.4.3 and 1.4.4)
>
> **Summary.** On Muse 1.4.3-R5018.1 and 1.4.4-R5419.1 (macOS arm64), `session/fork` with `cutPoint: { lastTurnId }` succeeds only when `lastTurnId` is the session's latest completed turn. Naming any earlier completed turn fails with `MSP error: invalid fork boundary for session …: InvalidCut` (`kind: "forkBoundaryInvalid"`, `retryable: false`). The stable schema (`muse schema generate-ts`, `ForkCutPoint`) documents `lastTurnId` as "the last completed turn to copy, inclusive"; only in-progress or unknown turns should fail.
>
> **Reproduction (no inference spend).** Point `muse serve` at a local OpenAI-Responses-compatible stub that answers every request with a short text reply. Start a session, complete three turns (`first`, `second`, `third`), record their turn ids from `turn/started`, then on a fresh connection call `session/read` (`lastTurn` is the third turn, `terminal: "completed"`) and `session/fork` with each id:
>
> | `cutPoint.lastTurnId`      | Result                               |
> | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
> | turn 1 (completed)         | `forkBoundaryInvalid` / `InvalidCut` |
> | turn 2 (completed)         | `forkBoundaryInvalid` / `InvalidCut` |
> | turn 3 (latest, completed) | fork created                         |
>
> Forking without a `cutPoint` also succeeds. The turns spawn the usual reminder-child lanes, which may relate to #55, but the error here is `InvalidCut`, the latest turn works, and no write fails.
>
> **Expected.** Any completed turn is a valid inclusive boundary, as on earlier hosts and as the schema states.
>
> **Impact.** ACP clients cannot branch a conversation from an earlier turn; the adapter (`@bex-co/muse-code-acp`) reports the limitation and does not emulate earlier boundaries.
