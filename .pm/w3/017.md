# 017 — Restored reminder cards stack above the replayed history

Why: after an editor restart, `session/load` sends restored worker cards before the history replay, so every past "Muse reminder: goal/verify" appears as a block of "…: completed" cards above the first prompt. That's noise that grows with each turn, out of order, with the status doubled in the title.

Estimate: 1h. Source: `/qa-find-bugs` verification run, 2026-10-26 (Muse 1.4.13-R6996.1, Bex 1.20; scenarios L5, R4, G5).

- Evidence: `analyze.mjs` "restored cards appear above the replayed history" (4 cards before the first prompt in L5 on the loopback; R4 on a real provider) and "status repeated inside a restored card title" (`Muse reminder: verify: completed`, 16×); the G5 screenshot showed the stack at the top of the restored thread.
- Cause: `loadSessionState` (`src/session-lifecycle.ts`) emits `restoredTaskUpdates` (`src/async-tasks.ts`) before `exportToUpdates`, and restores _completed_ worker items, including reminder children, whose titles already read `Muse reminder: <agent>` before `: <status>` is appended.
- Likely fix: restore only unfinished worker items (what `/tasks` would list), place them after the replay, and title them without the duplicated status. Verify with `/qa-find-bugs --only L5` (the finding must disappear) and a unit test on `restoredTaskUpdates`.
